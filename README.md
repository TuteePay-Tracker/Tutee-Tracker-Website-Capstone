# 🎓 Tutor Tracker (Tutor Tracker Capstone)

A modern, comprehensive monitoring, scheduling, and billing platform designed for private tutoring academies, bridging the gap between **Tutors** and **Parents**. 

Built with a robust, feature-first React architecture and real-time database syncing, **Tutor Tracker** simplifies academy management, automates tuition tracking, generates official receipts, and visualizes academic progression.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Personas

### 👨‍🏫 Tutor Portal
- **Student & Attendance Tracking:** Log and monitor attendance dynamically with statuses, session details, and subject coverage.
- **Automated Billing System:** Calculate session earnings, track outstanding balances, and monitor partial or full payments. Includes options to confirm G-Cash receipt attachments or proof-of-payment uploads.
- **Official Receipt Generator:** Click-to-generate and download official PDF/HTML receipts detailing transaction IDs, hours, coverage, and notes.
- **Academic Progress Reports:** File modular student evaluations containing subject score grids, action recommendations, and qualitative observation notes.
- **Announcements & Broadcasts:** Broadcast schedules, policy updates, or news directly to linked parents.

### 👩‍👦 Parent Portal
- **Aggregated Dashboard:** Get a quick birds-eye view of your children's tutoring stats:
  - **KPI Cards:** Aggregated attendance rate, total paid tuition, outstanding balance, and average assessment scores.
  - **Weekly Agenda:** Calendar overview showing class days, times, and tutor assignments.
- **Academic Progress Analytics (Interactive Charts):**
  - **Monthly Score Trends (Recharts Line Chart):** Track test performance curves over time.
  - **Subject Averages (Recharts Bar Chart):** Identify strong areas and subjects requiring extra support.
- **Child-Specific Detail Center ("My Children"):** Drill down into individual profiles to access progress history, review tutor observation notes, and download official payment receipts.
- **Live Tutor Chat:** Direct messaging interface allowing parents to communicate seamlessly with assigned tutors.
